Your questions, answered

Practical guidance for your property search. Transaction requirements depend on the property and emirate.

Can overseas buyers purchase property?

Dubai permits foreign ownership in designated freehold areas. Confirm the ownership rights for the particular property; Abu Dhabi has its own rules.

Official guidance ↗
What is the difference between ready and off-plan?

A ready property is completed; off-plan is bought before completion. Compare inspection access, payment timing, handover uncertainty and the final contract before choosing.

What costs should I budget for beyond the price?

Dubai sale registration generally totals 4%, with cost allocation confirmed in the agreement. Also ask for an itemised estimate of trustee, agency, mortgage and other applicable fees. Abu Dhabi fees differ.

Official guidance ↗
Can I use a mortgage?

Eligibility depends on the lender, your income, residency and the property. Seek pre-approval and confirm the deposit and total borrowing costs before committing. Approval is not guaranteed.

Official guidance ↗
Which documents will I need?

Expect identification such as an Emirates ID or, for non-resident buyers, a valid passport. Company purchases, representatives and mortgages require additional documents. Request a checklist for your transaction.

Official guidance ↗
How should I check an off-plan payment?

Verify the registered project and its escrow details through official channels before paying. Match the beneficiary and instalment to your signed agreement; do not rely on a forwarded payment message.

Official guidance ↗
How do I check a Dubai project’s progress?

Use DLD’s project-status service or Dubai REST, then request the latest developer update. A brochure handover date alone does not confirm current completion or readiness for occupation.

Official guidance ↗
What ongoing costs should I ask about?

Ask for the property’s current service charges, utilities, cooling arrangements, insurance and maintenance responsibilities. For retail, also check fit-out obligations and permitted business use.

Are the listed prices and payment plans current?

Document-based prices are reference quotations, not live inventory. Some offers have expired. We will request a current unit selection and written terms before you decide.

How can I arrange a viewing or consultation?

Use a project enquiry or send your shortlist through WhatsApp. Add a preferred date and time in Dubai time. Your advisor will confirm availability and the appointment; submitting a request does not reserve a property.
