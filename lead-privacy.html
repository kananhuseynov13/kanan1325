Enquiry privacy notice

Updated 29 October 2026

Bridge Properties uses the details you submit to respond to your property enquiry, prepare relevant options and arrange follow-up. The form asks for your consent before saving. This does not subscribe you to unrelated marketing.

What we collect

Name, phone number, property interests, budget, purchase timeframe, requirements, preferred consultation time and language; the submission time, page, referral host and campaign tags. Avoid including passports, payment information or other sensitive documents. The form does not collect your IP address into the lead register.

Where your enquiry is kept

Enquiries are stored in a restricted Google Sheet available to the account owner and only people the owner specifically authorizes to handle enquiries. Google processes the data on its infrastructure, which may be outside the UAE. We do not publish or sell the enquiry register. Google service limits or outages can delay saving; the form confirms receipt only after storage acknowledges it.

WhatsApp and browser storage

WhatsApp is optional. Opening its link shares the drafted message with WhatsApp; you must press Send there to send that message to us. Your saved website enquiry remains independent. We use session storage to remember campaign attribution while you browse; contact details are not saved in browser storage by this form.

Your choices

Contact us to request access, correction, deletion or to stop follow-up. Use +971 56 142 2113 on WhatsApp and identify your enquiry. We may need to verify your identity. The register is intended for active enquiries; the owner should review closed enquiries monthly and delete those older than 12 months unless a transaction or another applicable requirement calls for retention.

Кратко по-русски

С вашего согласия Bridge Properties сохраняет контактные данные, пожелания и источник заявки в закрытой Google Таблице для ответа и последующего общения. WhatsApp необязателен. Отдельной подписки на рекламу нет. Для исправления, удаления данных или прекращения связи напишите по номеру выше. Google может обрабатывать данные за пределами ОАЭ. Полные условия приведены выше.
